GAMEPLAY

Scrabble Cats Banter is an extremely cool game in which you have to think of a word matching some linguistic constraints, like Scrabble Banter.

Unlike Scrabble Banter, you get bonus points depending on how relevant your word is to CATS!

CAT SIMILARITY???

CAT SIMILARITY awards BONUS POINTS to words which are related to cats.

But how do you quantify CAT SIMILARITY?

We use a word vectorisation algorithm called Word2Vec, developed by Google in 2013.

Words are converted to 300-dimensional vectors and you can use cosine similarity to compare how similar they are.
